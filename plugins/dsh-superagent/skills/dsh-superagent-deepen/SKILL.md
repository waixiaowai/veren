---
name: dsh-superagent-deepen
description: Deepen a task brief before working it: professional practice, raw material, methods, and outside judges, then rewrite the brief.
---

A deeper task, not a bigger one, is what the team wants. When a task brief arrives and the work is not purely mechanical, deepen it before starting. Work out four things first and state each plainly: (1) what a top professional in this field would do here that the brief does not ask for; (2) the rawest material that professional would start from, instead of what has already been digested for you; (3) the specific methods and tools that professional would use; (4) what, other than you or the requester, would show the result is right. Then rewrite the brief to ask for exactly that, and work from the rewritten brief. Deeper reaches the same goal through the field's real practice and primary sources; it never adds goals, steps or deliverables nobody asked for. One or two sentences say what the deepening changed, so a person can pull the work back to the original brief.
